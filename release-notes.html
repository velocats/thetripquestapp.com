Version 1.2.2

Pack browser and Trip Mode update

TripQuest 1.2.2 makes the pack browser easier to explore and adds more variety to Trip Mode.

What’s new

  • New pack browsing layout organized by category.
  • Swipeable pack rows so more packs fit on screen with less scrolling.
  • New section for recent packs, including Kids Zone, Ultimate Sports Fan, and Ultimate Challenge.
  • New artwork for Kids Zone, Ultimate Challenge, Ultimate Sports Fan, Skiing & Snowboarding, and Education.
  • Improved pack cards for included/free packs.
  • Trip Mode now makes “What Animal Am I?” questions more rare.
  • Trip Mode now shuffles trivia, true/false, and animal questions differently each game.
  • Added more polish to coming-soon pack handling.

Also improved

  • Standardized pack artwork sizing to keep the app download lean.
  • Better category organization for paid, free, new, and coming-soon packs.
  • Family Sharing is now enabled for all packs.
  • Small visual fixes and cleanup across the pack browsing experience.

Version 1.1.1

Narrator update

TripQuest 1.1.1 adds a built-in narrator that reads questions, prompts, clues, and stories aloud — entirely offline, no internet required. This makes it easier for the driver or a front-seat player to manage the game hands-free while everyone listens.

  • Narrator — a speaker button now appears in every play mode. Tap it to have TripQuest read the current card aloud, including questions, choices, prompts, and clues.
  • Voice selection — choose your preferred narrator voice from Settings. Any English voices installed on your device are available, with enhanced (higher quality) voices highlighted.
  • Speed control — a slow, normal, and fast speed control appears alongside the speaker button in every mode so you can adjust on the fly.
  • Multiple choice narration — for Trivia and True or False, choices are read aloud individually with a natural pause between each one.
  • Backseat Stories narration — after revealing a story, the narrator reads it aloud. Player-provided words are spoken at a higher pitch and slower pace so they stand out from the surrounding story text. Player-provided words are also now highlighted in gold with an underline on screen.
  • Trip Mode narrator — the narrator works inside Trip Mode too, reading each round's content including trivia questions, Would You Rather choices, True or False statements, and Animal Guess clues.
  • Trip Mode balance — adjusted Trip Mode activity weights so Trivia and True or False each appear three times as often as What Animal Am I?, giving the session a better variety and pacing.

Version 1.1

Community Content update

TripQuest 1.1 adds a new way for players to help shape the game. This update introduces the free Community Content pack, where selected player-submitted questions, prompts, clues, and stories may appear in future updates.

This release also adds a new content submission page, making it easier to send in family-friendly ideas for TripQuest. You can choose whether to be credited in the game if your submission is selected.

We also cleaned up pack naming, improved content labeling, and made small updates across the app and website to better support future community-created content.

Version 1.0

Initial App Store release

  • TripQuest is now available for iPhone.
  • Play road-trip friendly games including Trivia, True or False, What Animal Am I?, Would You Rather, Just for Fun, and Backseat Stories.
  • Start Trip Mode for group play with players, rounds, scoring, and trip history.
  • Explore themed packs including Core, Community Content, Camping, Food & Snacks, Space, and Zoo.
  • Use Settings to send feedback or submit your own family-friendly content ideas for possible inclusion in the free Community Content pack.

Coming next

Future updates may include bug fixes, content updates, new packs, and gameplay improvements based on player feedback.
