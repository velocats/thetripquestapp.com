Version 1.0

Initial App Store release

  • TripQuest is now available for iPhone.
  • Play road-trip friendly games including Trivia, True or False, What Animal Am I?, Would You Rather, Just for Fun, and Backseat Stories.

  • Explore themed packs including Core, Community Content, Camping, Food & Snacks, Space, and Zoo.
  • Use Settings to send feedback or submit your own family-friendly content ideas for possible inclusion in the free Community Content pack.
