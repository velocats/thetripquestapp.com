Privacy

Privacy Policy

TripQuest is built for family-friendly play without accounts, advertising, or unnecessary data collection.

Last updated: April 29, 2026

Overview

TripQuest is an iPhone road trip game app. The app is designed to be played out loud by families, friends, and groups in the car.

TripQuest does not require you to create an account, does not include third-party advertising, and does not sell personal information.

Information the app stores on your device

TripQuest may store app settings and gameplay information on your device, such as selected preferences, saved trip history, player names you enter, scores, unlocked packs, and other app state needed for the game to work.

This information is used only to provide app features like Trip Mode, scorekeeping, history, content selection, sounds, haptics, and purchase restoration.

Information we do not collect through the app

TripQuest does not ask for an account login and does not intentionally collect names, email addresses, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone recordings, or other personal information through the app.

TripQuest does not use third-party advertising trackers.

Purchases and App Store information

TripQuest may offer optional paid content packs through Apple in-app purchases. Purchases, payment processing, refunds, and purchase restoration are handled by Apple.

TripQuest receives purchase entitlement information needed to unlock content in the app, but we do not receive your full payment card details.

Feedback, support, and email

If you contact us by email or use the feedback form on this website, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your message, your email address if provided, and any details included in your request.

We use that information to respond to support requests, review bug reports, consider content corrections, and improve TripQuest.

Children and families

TripQuest is designed to be family-friendly. The app does not require children to create accounts or submit personal information.

If a parent or guardian believes a child has sent personal information through email or the feedback form, please contact us and we will review the request.

Website analytics and hosting

This website may be hosted by a third-party hosting provider, which may process basic technical information such as IP address, browser type, pages requested, and server logs as part of normal website operation and security.

Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share information only when needed to operate the app or website, respond to your request, comply with the law, or protect TripQuest and its users.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as TripQuest changes. Any updates will be posted on this page with a new last updated date.

Contact

For privacy questions, support requests, or deletion requests related to information you have sent us, email thetripquestapp@gmail.com.
