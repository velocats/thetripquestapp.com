Featured mode

Trip Mode makes the whole ride feel like one big game.

Choose your content, set up players, and keep the trip moving with quick rounds. It is built for group play without making the phone the center of everything.

  • Great for families, siblings, friends, and mixed ages
  • Lets passengers play in short bursts or longer sessions
  • Keeps score without slowing down the trip

FAQ

Before the next drive.

A few quick answers before you pull out of the driveway.

What is TripQuest?

TripQuest is a family-friendly iPhone game app for road trips, group play, and casual get-togethers.

Is TripQuest only trivia?

No. TripQuest includes trivia, true or false, animal clues, would-you-rather choices, silly stories, and Trip Mode.

Who is it for?

Families, kids, parents, grandparents, friends, carpools, camping groups, and anyone who wants a shared game that is easy to start.

Where will I find it?

The site is thetripquestapp.com. You can download TripQuest from the App Store.
