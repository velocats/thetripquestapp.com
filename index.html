FAQ

Questions before the next drive?

What is TripQuest?

TripQuest is an iPhone road trip game app with multiple categories, quick-play prompts, and a Trip Mode for live group sessions with points and rounds.

Is it good for kids and adults?

Yes. TripQuest is designed for families and groups, with categories that work well across a wide range of ages.

Does it work on short drives?

Yes. You can open a single category for a quick burst of fun or run a longer session during bigger travel days.

What is Trip Mode?

Trip Mode is the structured group-play mode with points, rounds, and leaderboard-style play for the whole car.

Are there add-on packs?

Yes. TripQuest includes core content and can also offer themed add-on packs for specific trip types, destinations, seasons, or events.

Is it available on iPhone?

Yes. This website is built for the iPhone version of TripQuest.
